Verdict Build the LDO V2.4 R2 350, with a chamber heater. The biggest complete LDO Voron kit. A PTC chamber heater makes up for the big chamber on ABS, ASA, PA-CF and PC, with real safety hardware since Voron advises against extra chamber heat.
The tradeoff More cost, mains wiring and verification work, in exchange for big parts in engineering filaments on an open, cloud-free machine.
Out of reach for all three: PEEK and PEI/Ultem. Those need roughly 400°C+ hotends and a much hotter chamber, a different class of machine.
